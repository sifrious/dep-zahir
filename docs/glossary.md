# Glossary

## Account

The stable internal identity to which external login identities and product entitlements attach.

## External identity

The provider-scoped issuer and subject pair proven by an external identity provider and linked to one Account.

## Product

A connected application or commercial offering that defines named entitlements.

## Entitlement

A named, time-bounded grant allowing an Account to use one product capability.

## Identity provider

The external system responsible for authentication credentials, sessions, multifactor authentication, and recovery.

## Payment provider

The external system responsible for payment instruments, checkout, charges, subscriptions, invoices, and refunds.

## Product projection

A product-local record containing the stable Account identifier and only product-specific preferences or state.

## Trust center

The public Accounts surface that publishes approved legal, policy, security, accessibility, and compliance documents with stable URLs and version metadata.

## Accounts client

The reusable application package that initiates login through a selected identity adapter and sends authenticated requests to Accounts. It does not own credentials or duplicate account state.
