## Canceling a subscription

Customers may cancel an eligible recurring subscription through the Stripe Billing Portal or by contacting {{support_email}}. Cancellation becomes effective {{cancellation_effective}}.

## Access after cancellation

The account retains access according to the effective-cancellation timing stated above. Accounts revokes the paid entitlement when Stripe reports that the subscription no longer provides active access.

## Future charges

Cancel before the next renewal date to prevent the next recurring charge. Canceling does not automatically refund charges already completed; refund requests are governed by the Refund and Dispute Policy.

## Service termination

If access is terminated for abuse, security risk, nonpayment, or material breach, the effective date and any refund eligibility depend on the reason for termination and applicable law.
