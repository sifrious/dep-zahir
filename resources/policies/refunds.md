## Initial purchases

Customers may request a refund for an initial subscription purchase within {{initial_refund_days}} calendar days of the charge by emailing {{support_email}} with the account email and charge or invoice reference.

## Renewal charges

Customers may request review of a renewal charge within {{renewal_refund_days}} calendar days of the charge. Eligibility depends on account activity, prior refunds, and any rights required by applicable law.

## Duplicate, fraudulent, or incorrect charges

Contact {{support_email}} promptly about a duplicate, unauthorized, or incorrectly calculated charge. We will investigate using the Accounts and Stripe transaction records.

## Processing

Approved refunds are returned through Stripe to the original payment method. Bank and card-network processing times can delay when the credit appears. Refunds do not override rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.

## Disputes

Contact support before filing a payment dispute when possible. This gives us an opportunity to identify the account, explain the charge, or correct an error without delaying resolution through the card network.
