## Scope

This policy explains how {{business_name}} handles personal information through Accounts and the connected products that link to this policy.

## Information we process

Accounts processes identifiers supplied by the selected identity provider, account profile information, product-access entitlements, Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, support correspondence, and service security records. Connected products may process the requests, files, prompts, execution feedback, and artifacts a customer submits to that product.

We do not store complete payment-card numbers. Stripe processes payment instruments through Stripe-hosted payment surfaces.

## How we use information

We use personal information to provide accounts and product access, operate and secure the services, process subscriptions, respond to support requests, satisfy legal obligations, prevent abuse, and improve service reliability.

## Service providers

We disclose information to service providers only as needed to operate the services, including identity, hosting, infrastructure, execution, monitoring, support, and payment providers. Current providers that qualify as subprocessors are listed in the public trust center.

## Retention and deletion

We retain information only for the period needed to provide the service, maintain security and financial records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Product-specific retention details and deletion methods are published in the trust center.

## Requests and contact

To request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal information, email {{support_email}}. Available rights depend on the applicable law and the relationship to the service.

## Changes

We may update this policy when our products, providers, or legal obligations change. The published version and effective date identify the policy currently in effect.
