# Do not build

- Local password authentication. Revisit only if the external identity decision is reversed through an ADR.
- Card or bank-account storage. Revisit only if the external payment decision is reversed through an ADR and a security review.
- A generic authorization language. Revisit after three products require entitlement conditions that cannot be represented as named grants.
- Organizations, teams, invitations, or seat management. Revisit when two real people must share one account.
- Usage metering and invoices. Revisit when Logres has an accepted price tied to a measured unit.
- A customer portal. Revisit when the first paid plan exists.
- Bokonon person graphs. Revisit when authentication identity and curated-person identity have an accepted mapping.
- Product-domain settings. They remain in each product.
- Legal drafting or automated legal advice. Accounts publishes approved material; it does not approve it.
- Public disclosure of secrets, vulnerability details, sensitive audit evidence, or personal information.
