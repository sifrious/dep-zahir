# Do not build

- Password, passkey, MFA, recovery, provider-session, or credential storage.
- Payment methods or billing-provider state.
- Product profiles, preferences, roles, onboarding, sessions, or UI.
- Email-based account identity or automatic email matching/merging.
- Direct database access for product applications.
- WorkOS objects in Zahir's domain or public contracts.
- Speculative organization administration.
